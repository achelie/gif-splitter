questions.

File formats, frame quality, and what happens to your GIF.

How do I extract frames from a GIF?+

Choose a GIF above or drop it onto the upload area. The extractor turns its frames into PNG images. Click any frame to preview it, download that frame, or use Download all frames to save a ZIP.

Is this GIF frame extractor free?+

Yes. You can extract and download GIF frames for free, without creating an account. We don't add watermarks.

Are my GIFs uploaded to a server?+

No. Your browser reads the file and creates the images on your device. The sample GIF is downloaded from this website, but your own files are never sent to a server. See our privacy policy for hosting details.

Can I convert a GIF to PNG without losing quality?+

Extracted PNGs keep the GIF's original dimensions and decoded pixel colors. PNG compression is lossless, so we don't add compression artifacts or resize frames. It cannot restore colors or details already lost when the GIF was created.

Can I download just one frame or a selection?+

Yes. Click a frame and choose Download this frame for a single PNG. To save several, tick their checkboxes and choose Download selected. All PNGs in a ZIP are numbered by their original frame order.

Why is a GIF frame sometimes only a small patch?+

To reduce file size, GIFs can store changes between frames instead of full images. The extractor applies these patches and the GIF's disposal instructions to produce complete images, including transparent areas.

What are the file and frame limits?+

You can open one GIF at a time, up to 30 MB and 1,000 frames. Large dimensions or long animations can hit decoded-image limits before the file-size limit. If a GIF is too demanding for your browser, try a shorter or smaller version.

Does it work on phones and tablets?+

Yes, in modern browsers with JavaScript enabled. Tap Choose GIF to pick a file from your device. Large animations need more memory, so smaller GIFs work best on phones. Downloaded ZIP files can be opened in your device's Files app or an archive utility.
