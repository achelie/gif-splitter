Privacy policy

GIF Frame Extractor processes the GIF files you select locally in your browser. Your image files are not uploaded to our servers.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version

You can extract GIF frames without creating an account. This version does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The website's hosting provider still receives the information needed to serve the site.

Files you select

When you choose or drop a GIF into the tool, the browser reads it on your device, decodes its frames, and prepares PNG images and ZIP downloads locally. We do not receive the GIF, its filename, or the images you export through the tool.

The tool does not intentionally save your GIF files to a remote service. Downloaded files are stored wherever your browser saves downloads. Closing or reloading the page clears the current tool session; it does not remove downloads you have already saved.

Information involved in loading the website

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like other web hosts, Cloudflare processes connection and request information to deliver pages and protect its infrastructure. This may include your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time, and may be recorded in routine access or security logs.

This infrastructure processing is separate from extracting your GIF: the tool does not send your selected image to Cloudflare. You can read more about the host's practices in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This first version does not set application cookies and does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may use security-related technologies as part of delivering or protecting the website.

Accounts and payments

The tool does not require an account, a subscription, or payment. We do not ask you to provide a name, email address, or payment details to use it.

Links to other websites

If you follow a link to another website, that website's privacy practices apply. We cannot control how external websites handle your information.

Changes to this policy

If the website's data practices change, this page will be updated to describe them. The date above identifies the latest version of this policy.
